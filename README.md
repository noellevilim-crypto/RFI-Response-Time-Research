RFI Response Time Research

This repository supports a graduate research project titled A Retrospective Analysis of RFI Response Times on Commercial Construction Projects. The study examines historical Request for Information (RFI) records to identify response-time patterns and evaluate relationships between RFI characteristics, project characteristics, and response durations.

Research Data Management

Research data will be organized and documented to support consistency, transparency, and reproducibility. The analysis will consider variables such as RFI response time, project type, construction type, RFI category, and design discipline. Any data shared publicly will be anonymized to protect confidential project information, company identities, and individuals associated with the projects.

Data Availability and Licensing

The research uses historical RFI records obtained from commercial construction projects. Due to confidentiality requirements, the original datasets will not be publicly available. Only de-identified datasets, with project names, company information, and other identifying details removed, may be shared through this repository following review and authorization. Research documentation and original repository materials will be provided under the MIT License where applicable.
